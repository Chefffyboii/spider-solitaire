                                    Frequently Asked Questions

                                    Purpose of the game

                                    Remove all cards from the playing field in the fewest moves.

                                    Rules of the game

                                    The game starts with 15 columns of cards. Your goal is to arrange sequences of cards in descending order from King to Ace. Once completed, they will be removed to the foundation piles.

                                    1. Cards must be in descending order to move.
                                    2. Groups of cards can only be moved if they are of the same suit and in descending order.
                                    3. Any card or sequence can be placed in an empty column.
                                    4. When a sequence is completed (King to Ace of the same suit), it will automatically move to a foundation pile.
